# GNN E-Commerce Project - Problem Statement

## Business Context

Gaurab Nerpagar Numerologics (GNN) is a numerology-based spiritual products business that sells:
- Healing gemstones
- Crystal energy trees
- Numerology bracelets

## Problem Statement

### Core Challenges

1. **No Digital Presence**
   - Business relies on physical store and word-of-mouth
   - Limited reach to customers beyond local area
   - No 24/7 availability for product browsing

2. **Manual Order Management**
   - Orders taken via phone/WhatsApp
   - No systematic tracking of inventory
   - Payment collection is manual and error-prone

3. **Limited Product Information**
   - Customers cannot access detailed product information
   - No way to educate customers about numerology benefits
   - Difficult to showcase product variety

4. **Scalability Issues**
   - Cannot handle multiple customers simultaneously
   - No way to manage growing product catalog
   - Admin has no centralized system to update products

## Solution Requirements

### What the System Must Solve

1. **Online Product Catalog**
   - Display all products with images and details
   - Categorize by type (Gemstones, Trees, Bracelets)
   - Search and filter functionality

2. **E-Commerce Functionality**
   - Shopping cart system
   - Secure online payment integration (Razorpay)
   - Order tracking and management

3. **Admin Panel**
   - CRUD operations for products
   - Content management (sliders, reviews, galleries)
   - Secure authentication

4. **Customer Experience**
   - Responsive design for mobile/desktop
   - Fast loading times
   - Intuitive navigation

## Target Users

1. **Customers**
   - Age: 25-55 years
   - Interest in spirituality, numerology, healing
   - Tech-savvy enough to shop online
   - Looking for authentic spiritual products

2. **Admin (Business Owner)**
   - Needs to manage products without technical knowledge
   - Update content, prices, and inventory
   - View and manage orders

## Success Metrics

- Reduce order processing time by 70%
- Enable 24/7 product browsing
- Increase customer reach by 300%
- Centralized product and order management
- Secure payment processing with 99.9% uptime
